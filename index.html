Deposit recyclables seamlessly using your personal ATM-like contactless card and redeem accumulated credits for tangible everyday benefits.

Get Your ATM EcoCard

Each registered citizen is issued a personalized physical smart card with an embedded EMV chip, unique NFC UID, and secure tap-to-vend barcode.

2
📶

Tap at Any Smart RVM

Walk up to any EcoCash Reverse Vending Machine at campus or transit hubs. Simply hold your card against the contactless reader pad to start the session.

3
🤖

Deposit Bottles & Cans

Feed empty PET bottles or aluminum cans. The optical camera classifies polymers and precision strain gauges measure weight in grams.

Earn & Redeem Credits

Credits are instantly transferred to your card balance. Redeem them anytime for cafeteria meals, metro travel passes, and tree planting!

About the EcoCash Reverse Vending Platform

Empowering citizens through contactless smart card technology and automated reverse vending machines.

Closing the Plastic Loop with Instant Rewards

India produces over 3.4 million tonnes of plastic waste annually. Traditional collection relies heavily on unorganized scavenging. EcoCash transforms consumer habits by placing smart Reverse Vending Machines (RVMs) in colleges, metro stations, and office parks, paired with personalized ATM-style EcoCards.

Citizens tap their card, deposit clean empty containers, and earn guaranteed EcoCredits. These credits circulate in campus dining halls, metro transit gates, and eco-merchandise stores, creating a sustainable closed-loop ecosystem.

📶

RFID / NFC Tap Module

High-frequency 13.56 MHz contactless reader paired with EMV secure element that instantly authenticates the user's physical EcoCard in under 200 milliseconds.

📷

Optical Polymer Classifier

Dual high-speed optical vision cameras that distinguish transparent PET water bottles from rigid HDPE milk jugs and aluminum beverage cans.

⚙️

Internal Shredder Compactor

Heavy-duty rotary crushing rollers that compress bottles and cans by 85%, allowing up to 1,200 containers per machine bin before collection.
